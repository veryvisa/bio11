Concept slides概念幻灯

1/11

What counts as alive?什么算是生命？

Living things are made of cells, use energy (metabolism), grow, reproduce, respond to stimuli and keep a steady inside (homeostasis). Over many generations, populations also evolve.生物由细胞构成，会利用能量（新陈代谢）、生长、繁殖、对刺激做出反应并保持内部稳定（稳态）；经过许多代，种群还会演化。

2/11

Stimulus, then response先刺激，后反应

A stimulus is a change in the surroundings. A response is what the organism does about it. Sensing and reacting keeps an organism alive.刺激是环境的变化；反应是生物对它做出的行动。察觉并反应，才能活下去。

3/11

Homeostasis in one picture一张图看懂稳态

When conditions drift, the body senses it and corrects it. Body temperature around 37 degrees C is kept steady by sweating or shivering.条件偏离时，身体察觉并纠正。约 37 摄氏度的体温靠出汗或发抖维持稳定。

4/11

From cell to organism从细胞到个体

Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, organs form organ systems and systems form an organism. Each level does a bigger job.细胞组成组织，组织组成器官，器官组成系统，系统组成个体。层级越高，功能越大。

5/11

Beyond one organism超出个体

Organisms of one species in one place form a population. Different populations form a community. Add water, soil and sunlight to get an ecosystem.同一地点同一物种的个体构成种群；不同种群构成群落；再加上水、土壤和阳光就是生态系统。

6/11

Taxonomic ranks分类等级

From broad to narrow: Domain, Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species. Memory sentence: Dear King Philip Came Over For Good Soup.从大到小：域、界、门、纲、目、科、属、种。口诀：Dear King Philip Came Over For Good Soup。

7/11

Two-part names双名法

Every species has a two-part scientific name: Genus species, for example Homo sapiens. The genus is capitalized, the species is lowercase, and both are italic.每个物种都有两部分的学名：属名加种加词，如 Homo sapiens。属名首字母大写，种加词小写，两者都用斜体。

8/11

Three domains三个域

All life falls into three domains. Bacteria and Archaea are prokaryotes with no nucleus. Eukarya cells have a nucleus.所有生物分属三个域。细菌域和古菌域是没有细胞核的原核生物；真核域的细胞有细胞核。

9/11

Using a dichotomous key使用二歧检索表

Start at step 1. Read both choices, pick the one that matches your specimen, and follow it to the next step until you reach a name.从第 1 步开始，读两个选项，选与标本相符的那个，按指示进入下一步，直到得到名称。

10/11

Reading a cladogram读懂分支图

Each branch point is a common ancestor. Species that share a more recent common ancestor are more closely related. Read the tree by branch points, not by tip order.每个分叉点是一个共同祖先。共同祖先越近，亲缘关系越近。看分叉点，不看末端的排列顺序。

11/11

Derived characteristics衍生特征

A derived characteristic appears in one group and is passed to its descendants. Feathers mark the bird branch. Traits help us draw cladograms.衍生特征出现在某个类群并传给它的后代。羽毛标志鸟类这一支。性状帮助我们画分支图。
