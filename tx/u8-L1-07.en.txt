Quick check. Name a reptile. A snake, lizard, turtle, or crocodile. Fourth, the amniotic egg. This biological mechanism allowed reptiles to thrive on land. It let vertebrates reproduce on land, away from water. Finally, the bird. Showcasing further evolution and temperature regulation,
