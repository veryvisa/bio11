Memory hook, noto equals back, chord equals string. Quick check. In vertebrates, what replaces the notochord? Answer, the backbone.

The dorsal hollow nerve cord is a tube of nerve tissue along the back of a chordate. It becomes the brain and spinal cord. Memory hook, dorsal equals back. Quick check. Is the chordate nerve cord on the back or belly? Answer, the back or dorsal.
