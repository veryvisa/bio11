This is the brief on embryo layers and body cavities. First, the germ layers. Ectoderm is the outer layer, because ecto means outside. Quick check, what does it become? Skin and nervous system.

Mesoderm is the middle layer, meso meaning middle, forming muscle, bone, and blood vessels. Endoderm is the inner layer, since endo means inside, becoming gut lining and organs. Diploblastic just means two germ layers, because diplo equals two. Germ layers in a jellyfish? Just two. Triploblastic means three germ layers, with triplo meaning triple.

Are humans diploblastic or triploblastic? Triploblastic. Second, body cavities. A body cavity is the space between gut and body wall. Cavity just means hollow.

An acylomate has zero body cavity, since A means without. Flatworm body cavity? No. A pseudocolomate is partly lined with mesoderm, as pseudo means false, like a roundworm. A colomate is completely lined with mesoderm. Are humans colomates?

Yes. A colom, pronounced celom, is a true body cavity. Which layer lines it? The mesoderm. Finally, the big picture and test questions.

Ectoderm becomes skin and nerves. Mesoderm becomes muscle and bone, while endoderm becomes gut and organs. A-celomates have none, pseudo-celomates partly lined, and celomates fully lined. Test questions. Which layer forms nervous system and skin?

Ectoderm, because it's outside. Which forms gut lining? Endoderm, being inside. Jellyfish has two layers. Diploblastic, meaning two layers.

Flatworm has no cavity. A-celomate, meaning without. True celom is lined with. Mesoderm lines it completely.
