    How each block works每一段怎么做

    Each block is 25 minutes: check, study only what you missed, check again. Then stop.每段 25 分钟：先测，只学答错的，再测一遍，然后就停。

    1. Quick check: answer the questions. Press Again on anything you were unsure of.先测一测：回答问题，不确定的按「再看」。
    2. Study only what you missed: just those cards appear.只学答错的：只出现那几张卡。
    3. Check again: only the missed ones. Then tick Done and stop.再测一遍：只测答错的。然后打勾「完成」，就可以停了。
