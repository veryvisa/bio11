Chordates: from fish to birds脊索动物：从鱼到鸟

⬇ Download (m4a)下载（m4a） 📄 Transcript (txt)讲稿（txt）

Covers涵盖: chordate, notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, pharyngeal slits, fish, gill, amphibian, reptile, amniotic egg, bird

Transcript讲稿文字

First, the foundational chordate features. A chordate is an animal that at some stage has a notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, pharyngeal slits, and post-anal tail. Memory hook, chorda equals string. Quick check. Name the four chordate features. Answer, notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, pharyngeal slits, and a post-anal tail, at some stage. The notochord is a flexible rod along the back that supports the body of a chordate. Memory hook, noto equals back, chord equals string. Quick check. In vertebrates, what replaces the notochord? Answer, the backbone. The dorsal hollow nerve cord is a tube of nerve tissue along the back of a chordate. It becomes the brain and spinal cord. Memory hook, dorsal equals back. Quick check. Is the chordate nerve cord on the back or belly? Answer, the back or dorsal. Pharyngeal slits are openings in the throat region that become gills in fish. Memory hook, pharynx equals throat. Quick check. What do pharyngeal slits become in fish? Answer, gills. Second, aquatic and amphibious life. A fish is a vertebrate that lives in water, breathes with gills, and swims with fins. Memory hook, fish equals gills plus fins. Quick check. How do fish breathe? Answer, with gills. A gill is an organ that takes oxygen from water and releases carbon dioxide. Gills have many thin folds to give a large surface. Quick check. What gas do gills take from the water? Answer, oxygen. An amphibian is a vertebrate that lives partly in water and partly on land, with moist skin and eggs laid in water. Memory hook. Amphi equals both. Bios equals life. Double life. Quick check. Name an amphibian. Answer, a frog, toad, or salamander. Third, adaptation to land and air. A reptile is a vertebrate with dry, scaly skin that breathes with lungs and lays leathery eggs on land. Memory hook. Reptum equals crawling. Quick check. Name a reptile. Answer, a snake, lizard, turtle, or crocodile. The amniotic egg is an egg with a protective shell and membranes that lets the embryo develop on land. Memory hook. Amneon equals the membrane around the embryo. Quick check. Why did the amniotic egg matter? Answer, it let vertebrates in water and water. Reproduce on land, away from water. A bird is a vertebrate with feathers and wings that lays hard-shelled eggs and keeps a steady body temperature. Birds evolved from small feathered dinosaurs. Quick check. What covers a bird's body? Answer, feathers. Fourth, the ideas that tie these terms together. Four invertebrates. Insects have three body parts and six legs. Arachnids have two parts and eight legs. And crustaceans have two pairs of antennae and usually gills. Four chordates. Recap the four stages. Notochord. Dorsal hollow nerve cord. Pharyngeal slits. And a tail past the anus. Looking at the five vertebrate classes. Fish, amphibians, and reptiles are ectotherms. Birds and mammals are endotherms. On moving on to land. Amphibians need water for eggs. Reptiles evolve the amniotic egg with a shell to reproduce away from water. Finally, the test questions. Mollusk feature. Answer, mantle and muscular foot. Reason, snails, clams, and squid share them. Exoskeleton of chitin and jointed legs. Answer, arthropod. Reason, they molt it. Not a chordate feature. Answer, exoskeleton of chitin. Reason, belongs to arthropods. Which vertebrates are endotherms? Answer, birds and mammals. Reason, they make their own body heats. Animal with moist skin. Eggs and water. Metamorphosis. Answer, amphibian. Why is the amniotic egg most important? Answer, lets embryos develop on land. Reason, shell and membranes protect the embryo. Which group has mammary glands? Answer, mammals. Reason, mammary glands make milk. A kangaroo is a? Answer, marsupial. Reason, young develop in a pouch. Which structure moves in a sea star? The answer is tube feet, part of the water vascular system.
