First up, let's look at penicillin. It's actually the very first widely used antibiotic made from a penicillin mold, and Alexander Fleming first noticed it back in 1928. Here is the memory hook. Fermentum equals leaven. Quick check. What gas makes bread dough rise? Answer, carbon dioxide from yeast fermentation. Finally, we have mycosis. Answer, no. It is a fungal infection. Penicillin comes from? The answer is penicillium mold. Fleming discovered it in a mold. Bread rises because yeast produces? The answer is carbon dioxide.
