Concept slides概念幻灯

1/10

Four steps of natural selection自然选择的四步

1 Variation: individuals differ. 2 Inheritance: some traits are passed on. 3 More offspring are born than can survive. 4 Individuals with helpful traits survive and breed more.1 变异：个体各不相同。2 遗传：有些性状会传下去。3 出生的后代多于能存活的。4 有利性状的个体存活并繁殖得更多。

2/10

Peppered moth example桦尺蛾的例子

Soot darkened the trees. Dark moths were hidden from birds, light moths were eaten. Dark moths bred more, so dark became the common colour.烟灰把树干熏黑了。深色蛾不易被鸟发现，浅色蛾被吃掉。深色蛾繁殖更多，深色因此变得常见。

3/10

Individuals do not evolve个体不演化

Individuals do not change their genes to fit the environment. The population changes, because some alleles become more common across generations.个体不会为适应环境而改变基因；是种群在改变，因为某些等位基因在世代间变得更常见。

4/10

Microevolution and macroevolution微观与宏观演化

Microevolution is a small change in allele frequencies within one population. Macroevolution is big change over long time, including new species and new groups.微观演化是一个种群内等位基因频率的小变化；宏观演化是长时间的大变化，包括新物种和新类群。

5/10

How new species form新物种如何形成

A barrier, such as a river, splits a population. Each part collects different changes. In time they cannot interbreed. Then they are two species.河流等屏障把种群分开；各部分积累不同变化；时间久了无法相互繁殖，就成了两个物种。

6/10

Drift, bottleneck, founder漂变、瓶颈与奠基者

Chance can change allele frequencies, especially in small populations. A bottleneck shrinks the population; a founder group starts a new one with a small sample of alleles.偶然因素会改变等位基因频率，尤其在小种群中。瓶颈使种群缩小；奠基者带着一小部分等位基因建立新种群。

7/10

Convergent or divergent?趋同还是趋异？

Divergent: relatives become different. Convergent: non-relatives become similar because of similar environments.趋异：亲缘近的变得不同。趋同：亲缘远的因环境相似而变得相似。

8/10

Homologous or analogous?同源还是同功？

Homologous structures share an ancestor but may do different jobs. Analogous structures do the same job but come from different ancestors.同源结构有共同祖先，但功能可不同；同功结构功能相同，却来自不同的祖先。

9/10

Four lines of evidence四条证据

Fossils show change through time. Homologous structures show shared ancestry. Embryos of vertebrates look alike. DNA similarities match the family tree.化石显示随时间的变化；同源结构显示共同祖先；脊椎动物的胚胎相似；DNA 的相似度与亲缘树一致。

10/10

Artificial selection人工选择

People breed the organisms with the traits they want. Wild cabbage became broccoli, cabbage and kale this way.人们让具有想要性状的个体繁殖。野芥菜就是这样变成了西兰花、卷心菜和羽衣甘蓝。

Test practice测验练习

1. Which statement about evolution is correct?

  1. An individual changes its genes to fit its environment
  2. A population changes over generations
  3. Evolution always makes organisms stronger
  4. Evolution happens only in animals
Answer

B · Evolution is change in the inherited traits of a population over time.

2. Which is the original source of new alleles?

  1. Natural selection
  2. Mutation
  3. Competition
  4. Gene flow
Answer

B · Mutations create new alleles. Selection only sorts them.

3. In natural selection, "fitness" means

  1. strength
  2. size
  3. success at surviving and reproducing
  4. speed
Answer

C · Fitness is about leaving offspring, not strength.

4. Which is an example of an adaptation?

  1. A scar
  2. A muscle gained by training
  3. Thick fur passed from parents in cold climates
  4. A learned song
Answer

C · Adaptations are inherited traits that help survival.

5. Natural selection acts directly on

  1. alleles
  2. individuals
  3. species
  4. ecosystems
Answer

B · It favours individuals with helpful traits, but the population changes as a result.

6. Microevolution is best described as

  1. the origin of a new phylum
  2. a change in allele frequencies in a population
  3. extinction
  4. the formation of fossils
Answer

B · Small-scale change in allele frequencies.

7. A river splits one squirrel population into two. This is

  1. reproductive isolation
  2. geographic isolation
  3. a mutation
  4. artificial selection
Answer

B · A physical barrier separates the groups.

8. Sharks and dolphins have similar body shapes but are not closely related. This is

  1. divergent evolution
  2. convergent evolution
  3. coevolution
  4. speciation
Answer

B · Similar environments produced similar traits in unrelated groups.

9. A human arm and a whale flipper have the same bones. These are

  1. analogous structures
  2. homologous structures
  3. vestigial structures
  4. fossils
Answer

B · Same underlying structure from a common ancestor.

10. A bird wing and an insect wing are

  1. homologous
  2. analogous
  3. vestigial
  4. derived from the same bones
Answer

B · Same function, different origin.

11. After a hurricane kills most of an island's lizards, the survivors have fewer alleles. This is

  1. the founder effect
  2. the bottleneck effect
  3. gene flow
  4. sexual selection
Answer

B · A sharp drop in population size.

12. Which is artificial selection?

  1. Birds eat light moths
  2. Farmers breed cows that give the most milk
  3. A virus mutates
  4. A flood separates two groups
Answer

B · People choose which individuals breed.

13. Antibiotic-resistant bacteria become common because

  1. bacteria want to survive
  2. antibiotics cause the resistance mutation
  3. resistant bacteria survive and reproduce when antibiotics are used
  4. bacteria learn
Answer

C · Selection favours the bacteria already resistant.

14. A fossil with features of both fish and four-legged animals is a

  1. vestigial fossil
  2. transitional fossil
  3. index fossil
  4. mass extinction
Answer

B · It shows a link between two groups.

15. Which evidence compares DNA between species?

  1. Fossil record
  2. Embryology
  3. Molecular evidence
  4. Homology of bones
Answer

C · DNA and protein similarities show relatedness.
