Concept slides概念幻灯

1/10

Plant groups at a glance植物类群一览

Nonvascular: mosses. Seedless vascular: ferns. Seed plants without flowers: gymnosperms. Seed plants with flowers and fruit: angiosperms.无维管：苔藓；无种子的维管植物：蕨类；无花的种子植物：裸子植物；有花有果的种子植物：被子植物。

2/10

Why vascular tissue matters维管组织为什么重要

Xylem moves water up. Phloem moves sugar around. Lignin stiffens xylem. Together they let plants grow tall.木质部向上运水；韧皮部运送糖；木质素使木质部变硬。三者合作让植物长得高。

3/10

Leaf and stomata叶与气孔

The cuticle keeps water in. Stomata let CO2 in and oxygen and water vapour out. Guard cells open and close the stomata.角质层保水；气孔让二氧化碳进入、氧气和水蒸气排出；保卫细胞控制气孔开闭。

4/10

Alternation of generations世代交替

Plants alternate between a haploid gametophyte that makes gametes and a diploid sporophyte that makes spores.植物在产生配子的单倍体配子体和产生孢子的二倍体孢子体之间交替。

5/10

Who dominates?谁占主导？

In mosses the gametophyte is the main plant. In ferns, conifers and flowering plants the sporophyte is the main plant.苔藓中，配子体是主要植物体；蕨类、针叶树和开花植物中，孢子体是主要植物体。

6/10

Seeds change the game种子改变了局面

A seed protects the embryo with a coat and gives it stored food. Seeds let plants spread without needing water for sperm to swim.种子用种皮保护胚并提供储备养分。有了种子，植物不再需要水让精子游动，就能扩散。

7/10

Flower parts花的结构

The stamen makes pollen. The carpel has a stigma to catch pollen, a style and an ovary with ovules. Petals attract pollinators.雄蕊产生花粉；心皮有接收花粉的柱头、花柱和含胚珠的子房；花瓣吸引传粉者。

8/10

From pollination to fruit从传粉到果实

Pollen lands on the stigma. A tube carries sperm to the ovule. The zygote becomes an embryo, the ovule a seed and the ovary a fruit.花粉落在柱头，花粉管把精子送到胚珠；受精卵成为胚，胚珠成为种子，子房成为果实。

9/10

Monocot or dicot?单子叶还是双子叶？

Monocots: one cotyledon, parallel veins, parts in threes. Dicots: two cotyledons, net veins, parts in fours or fives.单子叶：一片子叶、平行脉、三基数；双子叶：两片子叶、网状脉、四或五基数。

10/10

Transpiration pull蒸腾拉力

Water evaporates from leaves through stomata. This pulls more water up the xylem from the roots.水从叶的气孔蒸发，把更多的水沿木质部从根部拉上来。

  4. seeds

B · Phloem moves food.

4. Which plants make seeds without flowers?

  1. Mosses
  2. Ferns
  3. Gymnosperms
  4. Angiosperms
Answer

C · Gymnosperms have naked seeds on cones.

5. Flowering plants are called

  1. bryophytes
  2. gymnosperms
  3. angiosperms
  4. conifers
Answer

C · Angiosperm seeds are inside fruit.

6. A plant with parallel leaf veins and one seed leaf is a

  1. dicot
  2. monocot
  3. fern
  4. moss

B · Monocots have one cotyledon.

7. Stomata are

  1. roots
  2. pores in leaves
  3. seeds
  4. flower parts
Answer

B · Gas exchange pores.

8. Guard cells

  1. make pollen
  2. open and close stomata
  3. carry sugar
  4. hold seeds
Answer

B · They control the stoma.

9. In the plant life cycle, the gametophyte is

  1. diploid and makes spores
  2. haploid and makes gametes
  3. diploid and makes gametes
  4. haploid and makes spores
Answer

B · Gametophytes are haploid and make gametes.

10. The zygote is

  1. haploid
  2. diploid
  3. a spore
  4. a gamete
Answer

B · Two gametes fuse to give a diploid cell.

11. The dominant generation in a fern is the

  1. gametophyte
  2. sporophyte
  3. zygote
  4. cone
Answer

B · The leafy fern is the sporophyte.

12. The male part of a flower is the

  1. stamen
  2. carpel
  3. ovule
  4. sepal
Answer

A · The stamen makes pollen.

13. After fertilization, the ovule becomes a

  1. fruit
  2. seed
  3. flower
  4. cone
Answer

B · The ovary becomes the fruit.

14. Water is pulled up a tree mainly by

  1. guard cells
  2. transpiration
  3. phloem
  4. root hairs pushing
Answer

B · Evaporation from leaves pulls water up the xylem.
